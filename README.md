SmartRetail PowerBI Project05
📌Project Overview:
    SmartRetail PowerBI Project05 is a retail business analytics project developed using Power BI to analyze sales performance, customer purchases, product            trends, promotions, profits, and quantity sold through interactive dashboards and visual reports.
    The project converts raw retail data into meaningful insights that help businesses monitor KPIs, evaluate promotional strategies, compare sales performance        across periods, and identify top and bottom-performing products.

📊 Dashboards Included
1️⃣ Sales Comparison Dashboard
  This dashboard compares:
    1.Total Sales
    2.Total Profit
    3.Total Quantity Sold
    4.Features
    5.Interactive date range slicers
    6.Comparative KPI analysis
    7.Visual comparison charts
    8.Period-wise performance tracking
    9.Insights
    10.Compare business growth across different time periods
    11.Analyze profit trends
    12.Monitor quantity sold performance
2️⃣ KPI & Sales Trend Dashboard
  This dashboard focuses on overall retail performance and promotional analysis.
  Features:
    1.Total Orders KPI
    2.Average Discount by Promotion Categories
    3.Profit vs Net Sales Scatter Plot
    4.Sales Trend Analysis Over Time
    5.Insights
    6.Understand promotional campaign effectiveness
    7.Identify relationship between profit and net sales
    8.Track sales fluctuations and growth trends
3️⃣ Product Performance Dashboard
  This dashboard analyzes product-wise performance.
  Includes:
    1.Top 5 Products by Sales
    2.Bottom 5 Products by Sales
    3.Top 5 Products by Quantity Sold
    4.Bottom 5 Products by Quantity Sold
    5.Top 5 Products by Profit
    6.Insights
    7.Identify best-selling products
    8.Detect low-performing products
    9.Analyze product profitability
4️⃣ Detailed Transaction Dashboard
    A detailed order-level dashboard containing:

📈 Key Insights Generated
    Apple iPhone 14 recorded the highest sales and profit.
    Weekend Flash Sale offered the highest average discount value.
    Strong positive relationship observed between Profit and Net Sales.
    Sales trends showed variations across multiple years.
    Some products generated low sales despite higher quantities sold.
